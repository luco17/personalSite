---
title: Getting cash out of UK ltd companies (2026)
date: 2026-08-25
---

Your company holds £5 million of surplus cash and you own all its shares. You can't bank transfer it out; it's the company's, not yours! Any payment to you—or another company you own—needs a lawful basis and may trigger tax. Dividends are currently taxed at up to 39.35%.

The alternative is a share sale: the buyer pays you, you pay CGT on the gain. Complete share sales attract premiums with diminishing premiums for controlling stakes, blocking stakes, etc. 


## Management Buy Out (MBO)
Management forms a buyer company that acquires some or all of your shares using their savings, outside equity, acquisition debt and/or deferred consideration. Acquisition debt pays you at completion; deferred consideration means the buyer pays part of the price later, usually from future business cash flow.

## Employee Benefit Trust (EBT)
An EBT can acquire and warehouse shares for selected managers, funded through borrowing, company contributions and/or deferred consideration. The trustees own and vote the shares until they transfer them to management.

## Merger or rollover
A buyer or joint-venture company acquires your shares for cash plus shares in the combined group. The cash element normally creates an immediate CGT charge; qualifying share-for-share rules can defer the gain on the rollover shares. The combined group can also reserve equity for its new leadership team.
